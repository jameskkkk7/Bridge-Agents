# Refunds and cancellations

This article explains the published policy for Widget Cloud and Widget Pro.
Support agents must not promise a refund that is outside this policy; eligible
cases are handled by support, and exceptional cases are reviewed individually.

## Widget Cloud — monthly plans

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. Cancellation takes effect
immediately for renewal purposes, and the workspace stays active until the end
of the current billing period. The remaining period is not refunded. Members
keep access until the period ends; after that the workspace becomes read-only
and the normal retention rules apply.

To cancel, an owner opens **Billing → Subscription → Cancel plan** and confirms.

## Widget Cloud — annual plans

Annual subscriptions can be refunded within 30 days of purchase when fewer than
five active members have used the workspace. "Active" means a member who signed
in or edited content during the current billing period.

After the 30-day window, or when five or more members have been active, a refund
is not guaranteed and is handled case by case. If a refund is approved, the
workspace is closed at the end of the process and data follows the standard
retention rules.

## Widget Pro

Widget Pro purchases can be refunded within 14 days of the first activation when
the workspace has used no more than one hour of sync time. Refunds are issued to
the original payment method and typically take five business days to appear.
After 14 days, or above one hour of sync time, a refund cannot be guaranteed;
support can review exceptional cases individually.

Activation codes are single use and expire after 14 days if never redeemed.
Unused codes are not refundable after the expiry date, but a replacement code can
be issued when the original was lost before redemption.

## Duplicate or failed charges

If a customer was charged twice for the same period, or paid after cancelling,
support verifies the invoice and requests a refund of the duplicate amount. Do
not ask the customer to open a chargeback before support has reviewed the
payment; chargebacks can suspend the workspace while the dispute is open.

## What to include in a refund request

- Workspace identifier and account email
- Invoice number or payment receipt
- Product (Widget Cloud or Widget Pro) and plan
- Purchase date and, for Pro, the first activation date and approximate sync time
- Reason for the request

Support confirms eligibility against this policy within one business day. If the
case is not eligible, explain which condition was not met and offer the
available alternatives (downgrade, cancellation at period end, or review by the
support lead).

## Cancellation help

For annual plans, cancelling stops renewal but does not trigger an automatic
refund. If a customer wants to cancel and also believes they are inside the
refund window, handle the refund review first, then cancel the plan according to
the outcome.
