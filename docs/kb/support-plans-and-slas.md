# Support plans and response targets

## Channels by plan

- **Starter** — email support during business hours.
- **Standard** — email support during business hours, with priority routing for
  workspace outages.
- **Enterprise** — named support contact, email and the private support channel,
  plus an optional dedicated Slack Connect channel.

Business hours are Monday to Friday, excluding regional public holidays, in the
support region assigned to the workspace. The portal displays current support
hours and the next holiday closure.

## First response targets

These are targets, not guarantees; complex issues may need longer to resolve.

| Severity | Definition | Starter | Standard | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 | Workspace unavailable or data inaccessible for all members; security incident suspected | 1 business day | 4 business hours | 4 hours, 24×7 |
| Sev 2 | Major feature unavailable or severe performance degradation; workaround is limited | 2 business days | 8 business hours | 8 business hours |
| Sev 3 | Individual feature broken; workaround available | 3 business days | 2 business days | 1 business day |
| Sev 4 | Question, configuration help or feature request | 3 business days | 2 business days | 1 business day |

Enterprise production issues receive a four-hour first-response target as
described in *Widget Cloud*. Response targets start when a ticket is received
through an official channel with enough information to reproduce the issue.

## How to report an issue

Include the workspace identifier, affected members, a description of the
expected and actual behaviour, approximate timestamps with time zone, and any
request or delivery identifiers from error pages or API responses. For
intermittent issues, note whether it happens on every attempt and in which
browser or desktop version.

Do not send passwords, API tokens, signing secrets or full customer records.
If support needs a specific record, share a screenshot or an export link with
the minimum necessary data.

## Escalation

Customers can request escalation when a Sev 1 or Sev 2 issue is not progressing.
Standard and Enterprise workspaces can escalate through the support ticket;
Enterprise customers can also contact their named contact. The support lead
reviews escalations during business hours and updates the ticket with an owner
and next checkpoint.

## Status page and maintenance

The public status page lists incidents and scheduled maintenance. Planned
maintenance is announced at least five business days in advance and is scheduled
outside the customer's business hours where practical. Emergency maintenance can
be announced with less notice when required to protect data or availability.

## Feature requests

Feature requests are logged against the workspace and reviewed by product
management. Support can confirm whether a capability exists and provide a
workaround, but cannot promise a delivery date. Security reports follow the
private vulnerability process instead of the feature-request backlog.
